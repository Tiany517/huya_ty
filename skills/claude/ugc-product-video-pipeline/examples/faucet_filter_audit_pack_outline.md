# Example Audit Pack Outline: 360 Rotating Faucet Filter

## Brief

- Product: 360 rotating faucet filter.
- Market: Mexico.
- Platform: TikTok.
- Language: Mexican Spanish.
- Consumer profile: Household kitchen user frustrated by fixed faucet direction.
- Aspect ratio: 9:16.
- User-provided selling points: 360 degree manual rotation, tool-free installation, three water modes.

## Safe Selling Angle

Show the inconvenience of fixed faucet water direction, then demonstrate the product making washing pots, fruit, and sink corners easier.

## Example Hook

Spanish:

```text
¿Por qué sigo moviendo la olla cada vez que lavo?
```

English meaning:

```text
Why do I keep moving the pot every time I wash?
```

## Product Constraint Example

```text
Product must remain palm-sized, attached to faucet head, manually rotated by hand, no automatic rotation, no floating product, realistic water physics.
```

## Beat Structure

1. Hook: user struggles with fixed faucet.
2. Reveal: product shown in palm and installed without tools.
3. Demo: rotate water direction manually.
4. Demo: soft spray for fruit, stronger flow for sink.
5. CTA: small change, more convenience.
