# Review Checklist

Approve these before generation:

- Product visual constraints are correct.
- Product size and shape constraints are strict enough.
- Target market and language are correct.
- Consumer profile is realistic.
- Selling points are accurate.
- Risky or unsupported claims are removed or approved with evidence.
- Script sounds natural for the target market.
- Storyboard actions can be shown on camera.
- Image prompts do not request text in frames.
- Video prompts request speech and mouth movement, not clip-level BGM.
- Final video should use actual-last-frame chaining.
