# Localization Rules

## Principle

Rewrite for the market. Do not directly translate.

## Chinese

Use when market is China mainland or the user asks for Chinese.

Style:

- Short, direct, benefit-first.
- Natural short-video selling language.
- Good for Douyin and Xiaohongshu.

Avoid:

- Overly formal advertising copy.
- Long technical explanation.
- Unsupported absolute claims.

## English

Use when market is United States or English-speaking TikTok/Reels/Shorts.

Style:

- Conversational.
- Creator-style first-person is acceptable.
- Show the problem before the product.
- Keep sentences short.

Avoid:

- Infomercial tone.
- Corporate brand-speak.
- Too many adjectives without demonstration.

## Mexican Spanish

Use when market is Mexico or the user asks for Mexican Spanish.

Style:

- Natural Mexican Spanish.
- Conversational TikTok UGC.
- Pain point should sound everyday.
- CTA should be soft and natural.

Avoid:

- Spain-specific phrasing.
- Literal Chinese/English translation.
- Overly formal neutral Spanish.

## Subtitle Rules

- Subtitle language should match the spoken script.
- Max two lines.
- Keep lines short enough for mobile.
- Avoid dense legal or technical wording in subtitles.
